## Fonts

All three are free (SIL Open Font License 1.1). The files in the preview come from `@fontsource` 5.3.0.

| Font | Brand use | Website use |
|---|---|---|
| Cinzel Decorative Black | The name "Christmas Nights" | The big title, the logo text, the footer name |
| Cinzel (Medium, Bold) | Headings and small spaced labels | Headings, buttons, labels |
| EB Garamond (Regular, Italic, SemiBold) | Body text | Body text |

Cinzel and EB Garamond have **no Kurdish or Arabic letters**. The Kurdish and Arabic pages need other fonts (open point in plan 005).

## Brand habits the website keeps

- A thin line with a small red diamond in the middle.
- A red line at the side of a short text block.
- Small spaced capital labels in Cinzel (only where the brand has them).
- Stamps: on the website they sit a little crooked, like on a letter.
- The edge of a stamp (a row of small half circles) is used where the night ends and the paper begins, and around the green sections.
