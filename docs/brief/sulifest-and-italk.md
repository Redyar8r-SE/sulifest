# SuliFest and italk — facts for the website

Gathered by the PM on 2026-11-27, because the site owner asked the page to talk about three things separately:
first **SuliFest** (the brand), then **italk** (the organizer), then **Christmas Nights** (this festival).

Only public sources are used. Numbers come from the organizers, as reported; nobody outside checked them.
**italk must approve every sentence before it goes live.**

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|

Numbers to be careful with:

| Number | What it really is |
|---|---|
| "500,000+ visitors" | A forecast made before the festival, not a result |
| "Nearly 100,000 visitors in the first four days" | The organizers' own count, reported by Channel8 on 1 July 2026 |
| Final visitor number for all 8 days | **Not found.** Ask italk |

So the page does **not** show a visitor number until italk gives the final one.

## 2. italk — the organizer

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| A youth-led organization, founded in 2019 | italk.krd |
| Non-governmental and non-profit, based in Sulaymaniyah | italk.krd |
| It started as small, informal meetings in coffee shops | italk.krd, "Our story" |
| What it does: conferences (the "iTALK Conference"), seminars and workshops, to share knowledge and experience | italk.krd |
| Mission, in short: give people in Sulaymaniyah open places to learn, talk and meet | italk.krd, "Our mission" |
| Values: community first, knowledge sharing, inclusive growth, transparency | italk.krd |
| It follows the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, mostly education, partnerships, less inequality, peace | italk.krd |
| It organizes SuliFest, and now Christmas Nights | sulifest.com; the Christmas Nights brief |

Not found: the number of events, speakers and people reached (the counters on italk.krd were empty when read), and the names of the team. Ask italk.

Spelling: italk's own website writes "iTalk". The Christmas Nights brand guide and brief write "italk". The website follows the brand guide: **italk**.
